Character bios

Meet the trio, the cult, the Crowned, and the people caught in the middle.

The Trio Blue - THE QUIET ONE 24 • Green eyes VIEW BIO +

Blue is the quiet member of the trio, but she is far from a background presence. At twenty-four, she has an unmistakable style: dyed-blue hair, a toque, and army boots. Beside Bug and Goose, her quieter manner gives the group a different rhythm, especially when their latest decision is threatening to become their next disaster. As the friends become entangled in the mysteries of Stoon, Blue faces the same frightening discoveries without the same need to fill every silence. The cemetery, the chapel, and the secrets beneath the city push all three beyond familiar ground. Her place in the story is not defined by being the loudest person in a room. It is defined by being there when friendship stops being easy and surviving together starts demanding more than any of them expected.

  • Dyed-blue hair
  • 5 ft
  • 120 lb
The Trio Bug - THE FIREBUG 23 • Blue-grey eyes VIEW BIO +

Bug has never been particularly good at staying out of trouble. At twenty-three, his fascination with fire has already earned him a nickname that follows him everywhere. With a backward cap, a restless streak, and a BMX for getting around Stoon, he meets danger with the sort of curiosity that makes his friends nervous. The outbreak gives that recklessness a much larger stage. Alongside Goose and Blue, Bug is drawn into a mystery involving the dead, a secretive cult, and a city coming apart around them. He brings nerve and unpredictability to the group, but acting first can make an already dangerous situation worse. When the streets turn hostile, the question is whether his appetite for chaos will help his friends survive—or give them one more fire to put out.

  • Dirty-blond hair
  • 5 ft 6 in
  • 135 lb
The Trio Goose - THE LIVE WIRE 25 • Brown eyes VIEW BIO +

Goose is difficult to overlook. Twenty-five years old, six feet six, and usually dressed in a basketball jersey, he brings a larger-than-life presence to a group that rarely gets a quiet moment. His curly hair and expressive face make his reactions hard to miss; his nickname began as an inside joke about getting angry like a goose. Where Bug supplies the sparks and Blue keeps things quieter, Goose brings restless energy. He is part of the unlikely trio pulled into the darker corners of Stoon, where a bad night can quickly become something much worse. His open reactions give the group some of its humor, but a world full of listening enemies does not always reward being conspicuous. Sticking together becomes increasingly important as the friends discover just how much they do not understand.

  • Curly brown hair
  • 6 ft 6 in
  • 250 lb
Other Uncle - THE BOOK WORM Details unrevealed VIEW BIO +

Uncle owns Olde Books, a Victorian bookstore with the kind of atmosphere that makes an ordinary afternoon feel like the beginning of a mystery. Behind the shelves is a man with an interest in questions most people would rather leave alone. His office, with its conspiracy-covered corkboard, is a fitting place for the trio to start looking for answers. As Bug, Goose, and Blue encounter the stranger side of the outbreak, Uncle becomes the leader they turn to. He helps connect their discoveries to a larger picture, guiding them toward the museum, the cemetery, and the unsettling history surrounding the Tomes. But understanding a threat and escaping it are very different problems. Whether he is studying a clue or getting behind the wheel of a rescue car, Uncle has to bring his knowledge into the dangerous world outside his shop.

The Crowned Marcus “King” Kane Early twenties • Gang leader VIEW BIO +

Marcus looks at a collapsing Stoon and sees a city waiting for someone to take control. As leader of the Crowned, he turns survival into territory, supplies, checkpoints, and influence. The crooked crown painted on a building is more than a signature: it announces that someone has claimed the street and expects that claim to be respected. Charismatic and ruthless, Marcus understands that frightened people need resources and structure. He also understands how much power belongs to whoever controls them. His ambition makes him a force in a city where the old rules are disappearing, but it draws him toward enemies whose plans reach well beyond a gang’s borders. Building a kingdom among the dead is one challenge. Recognizing when someone else is trying to use that kingdom is another.

  • Tall and lean
  • Black-and-purple streetwear
  • Orange crown emblem
