ABOUT THE GRAPHIC NOVEL

Welcome to Stoon.

The dead are rising. The city is falling. And the people still breathing have their own plans for what comes next.

Stories of Stoon is a black-and-white graphic novel blending cartoon horror, mystery, adventure, and offbeat humor. Beneath the chaos of a zombie outbreak lies a city full of buried secrets, dangerous ambitions, and people who refuse to go quietly. Survival is only the beginning of the story.

Bug, Goose, and Blue are three unlikely friends trying to make sense of a world that has stopped making sense. Bug brings a dangerous fascination with fire, Goose brings restless energy, and Blue is the quiet one who has to contend with the chaos around her. Alongside Uncle, the owner of Olde Books, they begin uncovering a mystery that leads far beyond the streets they know.

In the shadows, a secretive cult works beneath the cemetery. Jeb the Older, Cyrus, and Igor each play a part in plans that make the walking dead more than a disaster to escape. As the friends follow the clues, the line between the outbreak and the people behind it grows harder to ignore.

Elsewhere, Marcus and the Crowned see a chance to claim the city. Their crooked crown marks territory, loyalty, and a new kind of order built in the ruins. While some people search for answers, others are building kingdoms—and neither side can afford to overlook what is happening underground.

From the shelves of Olde Books to barricaded streets and the depths beneath the chapel, Stoon is a place where every discovery brings another question. With expressive characters, eerie settings, and humor in the middle of disaster, the story asks what people hold onto when their world falls apart—and what they are willing to become to take control of it.
